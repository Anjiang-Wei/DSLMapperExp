This package contains the Mapple mapper implementation and application sources
used with Legion/Regent and distributed tensor-algebra benchmarks. Original
copyright headers and third-party source attributions are retained.

Files bearing an Apache 2.0 notice retain that license; its text is in
`LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt`. The distributed tensor benchmark sources retain the
TACO license in `LICENSES/TACO-MIT.txt`. Generated parser and scanner files
retain their generator notices and exception text.

The Legion runtime, Regent toolchain, CUDA toolkit, BLAS and HDF5 are external
dependencies and are not redistributed in this package.
